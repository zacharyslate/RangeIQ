        Where the data comes from

          RangeIQ checks these sources every night. A source marked "not available" is left out of the pasture status instead of being guessed.

          How pasture status works

          Each pasture is rated from what was actually measured: rain compared with the same months in past years, satellite greenness compared with normal for the season, the U.S. Drought Monitor, long-term grass and bare-ground trends, and how long cattle have been in it.

          Good: no warning signs. Watch: one thing is off. Act: two or more things are off. These are starting thresholds; your own eyes on the grass come first.
