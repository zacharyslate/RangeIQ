RangeIQ

The ranch app for your phone

See how each pasture is doing, how much rain you've had against normal, weather alerts and fire danger, and your ranch in 3D from USGS lidar. Log a move or a rain gauge reading in two taps, even with no signal.

Open RangeIQ Desktop console

iPhone

  1. Open this page in Safari.
  2. Tap Open RangeIQ, then sign in.
  3. Tap the Share button, then Add to Home Screen.

Android

  1. Open this page in Chrome.
  2. Tap Open RangeIQ, then sign in.
  3. Tap Install at the top, or the menu ⋮ → Install app.

On a computer?

Point your phone's camera here to open the app on your phone.

Your ranch data stays private

This page and the app's code are hosted on GitHub. Your pasture boundaries, logs and ranch data are not. They stay on the RangeIQ server and are only sent to your phone after you sign in.

Data: National Weather Service, NASA POWER, U.S. Drought Monitor, Sentinel-2 (Earth Search), Rangeland Analysis Platform, USGS 3DEP lidar and The National Map, USDA NAIP imagery. Map: Leaflet. 3D: three.js.
